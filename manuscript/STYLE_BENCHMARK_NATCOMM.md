# Language benchmark for MAIN_TEXT_V3

Primary prose benchmark:

Song, Y. et al. *Machine learning guided formulation design of digital light processing printable elastomers beyond viscosity stretchability tradeoff*. **Nature Communications** 17, 7167 (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-73735-4.

This paper was used only as a high-level writing benchmark. No wording is copied.

## Traits adopted

1. Lead each section with the materials problem or result, not with methodological self-description.
2. Use a challenge -> approach -> quantitative result -> physical implication sequence.
3. Put the strongest numerical evidence close to the claim it supports.
4. Separate observed results from mechanistic interpretation.
5. Keep caveats local and specific instead of repeatedly defending the study.
6. Present machine learning / AI as part of the formulation workflow rather than as the scientific result itself.
7. Prefer declarative Results subheadings that state what was learned.
8. Keep the Abstract focused on one problem, one physical representation, one validation result and one broader implication.

A secondary structural reference is Szymanski et al., *Nature* 624, 86-91 (2023), which similarly places domain knowledge, explicit constraints and experimental adjudication ahead of generic claims about AI autonomy.

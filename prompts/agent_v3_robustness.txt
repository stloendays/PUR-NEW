This stage is not an architecture benchmark and not an ablation. It is a decision-quality gate before the final Judge.

Assess:

- whether the Planner's experiment intent is performance_mitigation or causal_isolation, and whether the modifier-axis complexity matches that intent;
- for performance mitigation, whether an independently supported dual-axis candidate gives a stronger test without requiring an unnecessarily large total modifier burden;

- whether it remains interpretable given process-state uncertainty;
- whether the recommendation depends on one fragile score or unsupported assumption;
- whether a nearby alternative would provide a cleaner test of the same discovered rule;
- whether the measurement plan is sufficient to adjudicate the intended claim;
- whether the Skeptic identified a problem serious enough to rerank, choose an uncertainty probe, or abstain.

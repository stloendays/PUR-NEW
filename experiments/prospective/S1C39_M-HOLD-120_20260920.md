## 4. Laboratory protocol frozen before execution

Use the verified laboratory platform already used for the manuscript:

- Shanghai Fangrui RV-SSR-H high-temperature rotational viscometer
- NKY-25 viscosity-heater unit
- No. 27 spindle
- viscosity output in mPa·s
- variable rotation speed adjusted to maintain approximately 40-60% torque
- sample held at 120 °C under stirring and sealed vacuum conditions
- prepared material sampled for viscometer measurement; not continuous in-situ sensing

Preparation backbone:

1. Charge PPG2000 and PDP-70.
2. Incorporate AC1920 using the same modifier-addition order used for the laboratory's existing resin-modified validation preparation. The exact modifier addition time/order must be written into the run sheet before the first S1C39 batch and then held fixed for all S1C39/S1C41 repeats.
3. Stir and vacuum-dehydrate the non-isocyanate mixture at approximately 130 °C for 1 h.
4. Add 4,4'-MDI.
5. Stir under vacuum at approximately 120 °C for about 1 h 20 min.
6. Define hold t=0 when the prepared sample reaches 120 °C.
7. Record viscosity at 15, 30, 45 and 60 min. Keep the material stirred and sealed under vacuum between sampling points.

No 90 min point is required for the primary hypothesis test.

## 5. Primary calculation

For each independent synthesis repeat:

[
SI_{15\rightarrow60} = 100\times\frac{\eta_{60}-\eta_{15}}{\eta_{15}}.
]

Record both the signed value and its absolute magnitude. The manuscript-facing primary summary is the mean absolute drift across independent synthesis repeats.

## 6. Frozen pre-result hypothesis predictions

The S1C39 reactive-core mass fraction is 0.85. Using the frozen E1 reference drift of 9.51%:

[
SI_{\mathrm{H-CORE}} = 9.51\%\times0.85 = 8.08\%.
]

The frozen hypothesis registry defines superlinear resin suppression as drift below one-half of the proportional-dilution prediction:

[
0.5\times8.08\%=4.04\%.
]

The frozen matched-window discrimination resolution is 2.0 percentage points.

### Predeclared interpretation

**Outcome A - acrylic-only reaches the low-drift regime**

If mean absolute S1C39 drift is **<=4.04%**, H-RESIN is supported and H-DUAL is falsified at the formulation level: the tackifier axis is not required to reach the low-drift regime.

**Outcome B - acrylic-only does not reach the low-drift regime**

If mean absolute S1C39 drift is **>=6.08%** and the contemporaneous S1C41 control remains in the low-drift regime (**<=3.80%**, one-half of its own H-CORE prediction), the result supports H-DUAL: the minor tackifier axis is required for strong drift suppression under this local formulation design.

**Outcome C - indeterminate zone**

If mean absolute S1C39 drift lies between **4.04% and 6.08%**, or if the matched S1C41 control fails to remain low-drift, the prospective experiment is reported as unresolved. Perform one additional independent S1C39 synthesis repeat without changing the thresholds or formulation.
